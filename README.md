A one-page newspaper front page summing up Thursday 26 November
2026's world news in a single image.

Six main stories: Hong Kong marking one year since the Wang Fuk
Court fire that killed 168 people, the deadliest blaze in the city
since 1948; the BBC's analysis of leaked Russian state statistics
counting at least 490,334 Russian soldiers killed in Ukraine since
2022; Shanghai party chief Chen Jining — once tipped for China's
top leadership — placed under a graft investigation, the fourth
Politburo member probed since 2025; Kilmar Abrego Garcia landing
in Spain after leaving the US voluntarily, ending a two-year
deportation battle; the Trump administration preparing to unveil a
new tariff plan; and world chess champion Gukesh defending his
crown against Javokhir Sindarov in Geneva. Ticker: North Korea
ordering Switzerland to shut its Pyongyang office, Bangladesh
garment workers winning a 36% pay rise, six dead in a plane crash
off the Mississippi coast, Thanksgiving Day in the United States,
Real Madrid thumping PSV with a Bellingham wondergoal, and India
marking Constitution Day.

The hero illustration was generated by the builder session (no text
in it — Tai Po's towers at dusk, candles and flowers laid in
memory); all headlines, the date "THURSDAY, 26 NOVEMBER 2026" and
captions were drawn programmatically (Python + PIL, DejaVu fonts)
so every word is crisp and legible. `main.py` copies the shipped
image to `/output/entry.png` after checking it is a valid PNG
within the size and dimension limits.
